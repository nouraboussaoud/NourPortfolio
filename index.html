MyBee — AI-assisted electric vehicle rental platform

Built for Akkurad (Tunisia) · Full-time engineering internship

MyBee digitizes the end-to-end rental of light electric vehicles for last-mile delivery: onboarding with OCR identity checks, booking and payment, electronic contract signature, live GPS tracking with geofencing, and return inspection with damage reporting. The standout piece is a conversational assistant that sits on top of the platform: it can answer questions, recommend a vehicle, quote a price, or pull up an invoice — grounded in the app's real data, never guessing.
